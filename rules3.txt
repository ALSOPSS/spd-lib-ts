Communication That Cannot Be Proven to Exist
Omega-Level Encryption is the extreme limit of secure communication.
The goal is not merely unbreakable encryption, but making it impossible for any observer to prove that communication occurred at all.
Even an attacker with:
infinite computation
full network surveillance
infinite storage
future technology
cannot determine that a message exists.
Security depends entirely on physics, statistics, and uncertainty.

Assume the adversary can:

Omega-Level Encryption hides communication inside the fundamental noise of the universe.
Core Principle
Normal encryption protects message content.
Omega-Level encryption protects message existence.
To an attacker, the world with communication and the world without communication are statistically indistinguishable.
Layer 1 — Infinite Noise Baseline
Every node continuously emits encrypted packets forever.
Example behavior:
Node traffic pattern:
send random packet every 10–100 ms
Packets are always:
random length
random timing
random routing
random encryption keys
Result:
Network traffic is pure entropy.
A real message is simply a tiny perturbation inside this ocean of randomness.
An observer cannot detect it.

This makes:
